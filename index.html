HDD Drilling Simulator — Support

A directional drilling game for iPhone: steer the bore, read the tracker, and don't strike the utilities.

Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feedback — email generalobvious87@gmail.com and we'll get back to you.

How to play

  • Tap TAP TO START and pick a level.
  • Hold above the bore head to steer up (12 o'clock), below it to steer down (6 o'clock). Release to drill straight.
  • Surface inside the exit pit to finish. Hit a buried utility and the bore is over.
